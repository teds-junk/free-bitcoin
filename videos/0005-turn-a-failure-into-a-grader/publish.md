## Description
We found the skill's failures by reading patches, and after every edit we'd have to read them all again. In this lesson we turn three of those failures into small graders that read the diff, one per failure mode, and let one command do the counting. We measure the skill as it is, add one rule per failure, and measure again. In our run the count went from 3 of 9 trials to 9 of 9 on one model and from 0 of 9 to 9 of 9 on the other; an earlier run of the same edit left one model at 8 of 9, which is why a count beats a glance at one patch. Lesson 5 of Vally & Agent Evals.

Source: lessons/0005-turn-a-failure-into-a-grader.html (Vally & Agent Evals). Vally docs: "Choose a Grader".
