## Description
We now have five ways to check the skill, and they differ enormously in cost. In this lesson we turn them into a routine a teammate can repeat: tag the stimuli that code can check, name a quick suite and a full suite, and make a failing verdict exit with an error so a script can stop on it. In our run the quick suite passed five of five on both models for about 24 AIU; a saved run with real failures, re-graded for free, exited one; and the full suite passed all thirty trials on the take we kept, after a first take with two dropped sessions and one real fail. Then we write down what the team agreed, on one page. Lesson 21 of Vally & Agent Evals.

Source: lessons/0021-keep-it-working.html (Vally & Agent Evals). Vally docs, "Authoring Eval Suites".
