## Description
The judge said yes to some entries and no to others. It is a model. Is it right? In this lesson we sort twelve labelled trials into four boxes and work out two numbers: of the entries we said yes to, how many the judge passed (TPR), and of those we said no to, how many it failed (TNR). One agreement figure would hide which way the errors run. In our run the judge matched eleven of twelve labels: TPR 7 of 7, TNR 4 of 5. We examine the one disagreement, two entries a single word apart that the judge split, and write down what we will and won't rely on this judge for, and what twelve labels can and can't show. Lesson 13 of Vally & Agent Evals.
