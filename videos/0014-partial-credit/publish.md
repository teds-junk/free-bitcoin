## Description
A yes-or-no judge can't tell an entry that is nearly right from one that is far off. In this lesson we look at how Vally turns grader results into a verdict, with and without a `scoring` block, then ask our judge for a score from 1 to 5 against a threshold and re-grade the same twelve saved trials. In our run the scale did separate the close entries from the far-off ones, and one verdict changed; against our labels the scaled judge caught three of five bad entries where the yes-or-no judge caught four. Then we add weights and a threshold and watch all twelve trials pass, four of them with a failing judge inside. Lesson 14 of Vally & Agent Evals.

Source: lessons/0014-partial-credit.html (Vally & Agent Evals). Vally docs: "Scoring"; "Grader: prompt". Husain, "Using LLM-as-a-Judge"; Anthropic, "Demystifying evals for AI agents" (2026).
