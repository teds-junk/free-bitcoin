## Title options

3. A budget is a grader: failing, or stopping, a trial that takes too much

## Description
The graders so far ask whether the work is right. None asks what it took. In this lesson we add budget graders for tokens, turns and time, and a limit that stops the agent, and we set them from our own runs instead of guessing. Budgets just above a typical trial failed three good trials of thirty when we re-graded saved trials for free, so we widened them to about twice the typical value, where they failed none; a live run then stayed well inside them. A budget judges the finished trial; a limit acts while the agent works. Lesson 18 of Vally & Agent Evals.

Source: lessons/0018-put-a-ceiling-on-a-trial.html (Vally & Agent Evals). Vally docs, "Graders: metric thresholds".
