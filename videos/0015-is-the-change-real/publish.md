## Description
After an edit, 7 of 10 becomes 9 of 10. Is that real? A count from a few trials has a wide range around it, and a change inside that range is not evidence. In this lesson we keep a copy of the skill, sharpen one rule, and run both versions on both models as four variants, then count trials and put the counts into a range calculator. In our run one model went from 5 of 9 to 9 of 9 and the other from 5 of 6 finished trials to 9 of 9; both pairs of ranges overlap, so these trials can't yet tell. We also look back at the course's earlier fixes and say which were measured and which were only reasonable. Lesson 15 of Vally & Agent Evals.

Source: lessons/0015-is-the-change-real.html (Vally & Agent Evals). Evan Miller, "Adding Error Bars to Evals" (2024). Vally docs: "Scoring Functions".
