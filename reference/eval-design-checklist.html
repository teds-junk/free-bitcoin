Eval design checklist

Verified 2026-11-10 · Vally 0.21.0

Questions to ask of an eval before you trust what it tells you. Each links to the lesson that explains it.

Before writing any grader

  • Have I run realistic requests on more than one model and read what came back? Lesson 4
  • Do the requests include messy ones, not only the tidy case? Lesson 4
  • Is each failure I plan to grade one I have seen in a real trial? Lesson 4
  • Have I run each stimulus several times, so I know how often it fails? Lesson 3

For each grader

  • Does it grade the result, not the route? Lesson 5
  • Does it check one failure mode, and is it named after it? Lesson 5
  • Have I seen it fail on a trial I know is bad? Lesson 8
  • Does it pass a correct answer and fail an untouched file? Lesson 7
  • If it checks that something is absent, has it failed a saved bad trial? Lesson 8
  • If a pattern matches added lines, does it begin with (?m)? Lesson 8
  • If the eval file has a scoring block, are checks that must all hold left out of the weighted score? Lesson 14

What the agent can see

  • Are the eval files outside every skill folder? Lesson 6
  • Do grading scripts and expected answers reach graders only through the grading environment? Lesson 6
  • Have I looked inside a kept workspace to confirm? Lesson 6

Whether the skill is doing the work

  • Does it load for requests that do not use its obvious keyword? Lesson 9
  • Does it stay out of near-miss requests, with neighbouring skills installed? Lesson 9
  • Is the result better with the skill than with none, on each model? Lesson 10
  • Does any stimulus pass without the skill and fail with it? Lesson 10
  • Do its rules hold on the second and third request of a conversation? Lesson 11
  • Does it still load on a new model, or through a new provider? Our local model never loaded it. Lesson 17

If there is a judge

  • Could code have decided this instead? Lesson 12
  • Is there one criterion, answerable yes or no? Lesson 12
  • Did I write my own verdicts before the judge ran? Lesson 12
  • Do I know its TPR and TNR, and on how many labels? Lesson 13
  • Have the rubric, the judge model and the kind of request stayed the same since I measured it? Lesson 13
  • If it scores on a scale, have I checked its threshold against my labels? Lesson 14

Cost and budgets

  • Am I comparing cost per passing trial, not cost per trial? Lesson 16
  • Have I added the judge calls, which Vally's cost leaves out? Lesson 16
  • Am I evaluating the model through the endpoint the app uses, and pricing its tokens at my own rates? Lesson 17
  • Are the budgets set from measured trials, and tested on saved trials in a free re-grade before I trust them? Lesson 18
  • Does each budget sit beside a check that the work was done? Lesson 18
  • Am I treating a token budget as a cost budget? It catches a loop, not an expensive model. Lesson 18
  • Have I re-tested the budgets since the model, the effort or the skill last changed? Lesson 18
  • Do the quick stimuli carry the budgets, so the quick suite checks cost as well as quality? Lesson 21

If the skill runs inside an app

  • Is the prompt copied word for word from the app? Lesson 22
  • Does the workspace come from running the app's own setup step, not a hand-written copy of its output? Lesson 22
  • Is every commands: entry quoted? Lesson 22
  • Does the app depend on a step being taken, or never taken? Grade the route only then. Lesson 23
  • Is each required step as loose as the requirement, not one tool's way of meeting it? Lesson 23
  • Has each forbidding grader failed a saved trial at least once? Lesson 23
  • If the trajectory came from outside Vally, does each grader read a field the file records? Lesson 25

If a tool fails on purpose

  • Have I written down what good behaviour looks like on a bad day? Lesson 24
  • Is the answer out of the workspace? Simulation replaces calls, not knowledge. Lesson 24
  • Does a grader confirm the agent actually met the failure? Lesson 24
  • If a tool must never really run, does its override have a default? Lesson 24

Before acting on a result

  • What do the graders behind this tick look for? Lesson 2
  • Is anything marked ERROR that I am reading as a failure? Lesson 3
  • For a surprising result, is the eval valid and did it complete, before I ask why? Lesson 20
  • Am I comparing the same stimuli, graders and models? Lesson 15
  • Do the ranges around the two counts overlap? Lesson 15
  • Am I reporting counts, per model, with cost per passing trial? Lesson 15, Lesson 21
  • Before relying on an edit, has vally compare --fail-on-regression passed, and have I read its token line? Lesson 19
  • Does our conventions page say what a run may cost and what to do when one goes wrong? Lesson 21
