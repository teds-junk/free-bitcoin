# Vally & Agent Evals

**Take the course: https://teds-junk.github.io/free-bitcoin/**

Learn to evaluate AI agent skills with Vally: take one changelog skill from "it works for me" to defensible evidence, with repeated trials across models, tested graders, a judge you've checked against your own labels, honest statistics and costs, and finally testing the skill inside a real app.

26 lessons, each with a narrated video, exercises, and the files and output from our own runs. The reference sheets (workflow guide, grader catalog, troubleshooting and more) are the part to keep.

The whole course is also available as [one video](https://github.com/teds-junk/free-bitcoin/releases/tag/course-video-v1), about 4 h 49 min.
